Child Safety & CSAE Standards

Snuger – People Near You

Last updated: June 2026

Our Commitment

Snuger has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and strictly prohibit any content, behavior, or activity that sexually exploits or endangers minors.

Prohibited Content & Behavior

The following are strictly forbidden on Snuger:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Grooming, solicitation, or exploitation of minors
  • Sharing, distributing, or requesting sexual content involving anyone under 18
  • Any communication intended to exploit, harm, or endanger a child
  • Using anonymous mode to contact or target minors inappropriately

Reporting Mechanisms

Snuger provides multiple ways to report child safety concerns:

  • In-app reporting: every post, chat, and user profile has a "Report" button accessible directly within the app
  • Email: users can report CSAE concerns to: support@snuger.in
  • Reports are reviewed promptly and escalate to authorities where required by law

How We Respond

Upon receiving a CSAE report:

  • The reported content is immediately reviewed
  • Content confirmed as CSAM is removed without delay
  • The responsible account is permanently banned
  • We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via CyberTipline and to relevant Indian authorities under the POCSO Act, 2012
  • All evidence is preserved and cooperated with law enforcement

Legal Compliance

Snuger complies with:

  • The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 — India
  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules
  • NCMEC CyberTipline mandatory reporting obligations
  • All applicable regional and national child protection laws

Updates to This Policy

This policy is reviewed regularly and updated to reflect changes in law, platform features, and best practices in child safety.

Designated Contact

For child safety concerns, CSAE reports, or compliance inquiries:

Email: support@snuger.in

Response time: Within 24 hours
